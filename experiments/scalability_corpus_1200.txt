### DOMAIN: GEOGRAPHY ###

[SB-0001] Nile River
Type: general knowledge
The Nile is a major north-flowing river in northeastern Africa. It is commonly associated with Egypt and Sudan and has long supported agriculture and settlement along its valley.

[SB-0002] Sahara Desert
Type: general knowledge
The Sahara is the largest hot desert in the world and covers much of North Africa. Its landscapes include sand seas, gravel plains, rocky plateaus, mountains and oases.

[SB-0003] Andes Mountains
Type: general knowledge
The Andes form a long mountain chain along the western edge of South America. The range passes through several countries including Chile, Argentina, Peru, Bolivia, Ecuador and Colombia.

[SB-0004] Great Barrier Reef
Type: general knowledge
The Great Barrier Reef lies off the northeastern coast of Australia in the Coral Sea. It is a large system of coral reefs and islands supporting highly diverse marine life.

[SB-0005] Lake Baikal
Type: general knowledge
Lake Baikal is a very deep freshwater lake in Siberia, Russia. It is notable for its great depth, age and large share of the world’s unfrozen surface freshwater by volume.

[SB-0006] Amazon Basin
Type: general knowledge
The Amazon Basin is the drainage basin of the Amazon River and its tributaries in South America. It contains the largest tropical rainforest on Earth.

[SB-0007] Japan archipelago
Type: general knowledge
Japan is an island country in East Asia composed of thousands of islands. The four largest are Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu and Shikoku.

[SB-0008] Mediterranean Sea
Type: general knowledge
The Mediterranean Sea lies between Europe, Africa and Asia and connects to the Atlantic Ocean through the Strait of Gibraltar.

[SB-0009] Iceland geology
Type: general knowledge
Iceland sits on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge and is also influenced by a mantle hotspot. This contributes to frequent volcanic and geothermal activity.

[SB-0010] Himalayas
Type: general knowledge
The Himalayas are a mountain system in Asia created by the ongoing collision of the Indian and Eurasian tectonic plates. Mount Everest lies in this system.

[SB-0011] Danube River
Type: general knowledge
The Danube is one of Europe’s major rivers. It flows generally eastward through or along multiple countries before reaching the Black Sea.

[SB-0012] Greenland
Type: general knowledge
Greenland is the world’s largest island that is not a continent. Most of its interior is covered by a large ice sheet.

[SB-0013] Atacama Desert
Type: general knowledge
The Atacama Desert in northern Chile is one of the driest non-polar deserts on Earth. Some areas receive extremely little rainfall.

[SB-0014] New Zealand
Type: general knowledge
New Zealand lies in the southwestern Pacific Ocean and consists mainly of the North Island and South Island, together with many smaller islands.

[SB-0015] Dead Sea
Type: general knowledge
The Dead Sea is a hypersaline lake bordered by Jordan to the east and Israel and the West Bank to the west. Its shoreline is among the lowest exposed land elevations on Earth.

[SB-0016] Mississippi River
Type: general knowledge
The Mississippi River is a major river of North America that drains a large part of the central United States and flows into the Gulf of Mexico.

[SB-0017] Gobi Desert
Type: general knowledge
The Gobi is a large cold desert region in northern China and southern Mongolia. Much of it consists of exposed rock and gravel rather than continuous sand dunes.

[SB-0018] Congo Basin
Type: general knowledge
The Congo Basin in Central Africa contains the world’s second-largest tropical rainforest after the Amazon rainforest.

[SB-0019] Alps
Type: general knowledge
The Alps are a major European mountain system crossing countries including France, Switzerland, Italy, Austria, Germany, Slovenia and Liechtenstein.

[SB-0020] Baltic Sea
Type: general knowledge
The Baltic Sea is a brackish inland sea in northern Europe connected to the North Sea through Danish straits.

### DOMAIN: HISTORY ###

[SB-0101] Printing press in Europe
Type: general knowledge
Johannes Gutenberg is associated with the development of movable-type printing in fifteenth-century Europe. Printing greatly reduced the cost of reproducing books and helped accelerate the spread of ideas.

[SB-0102] French Revolution
Type: general knowledge
The French Revolution began in 1789 and transformed political and social structures in France. It challenged monarchy and aristocratic privilege and promoted ideas of citizenship and popular sovereignty.

[SB-0103] Industrial Revolution
Type: general knowledge
The Industrial Revolution began in Britain in the eighteenth century and involved mechanized production, factory systems, new energy sources and major changes in transport and urban life.

[SB-0104] Magna Carta
Type: general knowledge
Magna Carta was sealed in England in 1215. Although created in a medieval political conflict, it later became an important symbol of limits on governmental power and legal rights.

[SB-0105] Roman Republic
Type: general knowledge
Before becoming an empire, Rome was governed for centuries as a republic with elected magistrates, assemblies and a powerful Senate.

[SB-0106] Fall of Constantinople
Type: general knowledge
Constantinople was captured by the Ottoman Empire in 1453. The event ended the Byzantine Empire and strengthened Ottoman control in the eastern Mediterranean.

[SB-0107] American Declaration of Independence
Type: general knowledge
The United States Declaration of Independence was adopted in 1776 and announced that the thirteen colonies regarded themselves as independent from Britain.

[SB-0108] Black Death
Type: general knowledge
The Black Death was a devastating plague pandemic in the fourteenth century that caused extremely high mortality across Europe, Asia and North Africa.

[SB-0109] Renaissance
Type: general knowledge
The Renaissance was a broad cultural movement associated with renewed interest in classical learning, new artistic techniques and intellectual developments in Europe, especially from the fourteenth through sixteenth centuries.

[SB-0110] First World War
Type: general knowledge
The First World War lasted from 1914 to 1918 and involved major powers in Europe and beyond. It caused enormous casualties and contributed to the collapse of several empires.

[SB-0111] Second World War
Type: general knowledge
The Second World War lasted from 1939 to 1945 and became a global conflict involving most of the world’s major powers.

[SB-0112] Moon landing
Type: general knowledge
Apollo 11 landed humans on the Moon in July 1969. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walked on the lunar surface while Michael Collins remained in lunar orbit.

[SB-0113] Berlin Wall
Type: general knowledge
The Berlin Wall was erected in 1961 and became a major symbol of the Cold War division of Germany and Europe. It opened in November 1989.

[SB-0114] Silk Roads
Type: general knowledge
The Silk Roads were networks of trade routes linking regions of Eurasia. They enabled exchange not only of goods but also religions, technologies, languages and artistic ideas.

[SB-0115] Ancient Egyptian writing
Type: general knowledge
Ancient Egyptians used several writing systems, including hieroglyphic script. Writing appeared on monuments, religious texts, administrative records and everyday documents.

[SB-0116] Viking Age
Type: general knowledge
The Viking Age is commonly dated from the late eighth to the eleventh century and involved Scandinavian raiding, trade, settlement and exploration across large parts of Europe and the North Atlantic.

[SB-0117] Meiji Restoration
Type: general knowledge
The Meiji Restoration of 1868 transformed Japan’s political system and began a period of rapid modernization, industrialization and institutional reform.

[SB-0118] Hanseatic League
Type: general knowledge
The Hanseatic League was a network of merchant towns and trading organizations that played an important role in commerce around the Baltic and North Seas during the medieval and early modern periods.

[SB-0119] Abolition of transatlantic slave trade
Type: general knowledge
During the nineteenth century, several states outlawed participation in the transatlantic slave trade, although slavery itself continued in many places after those bans.

[SB-0120] United Nations founding
Type: general knowledge
The United Nations was founded in 1945 after the Second World War with the aim of promoting international cooperation, peace and security.

### DOMAIN: TECHNOLOGY ###

[SB-0201] HTTP
Type: general knowledge
HTTP is an application-layer protocol used for transferring representations of resources on the web. HTTPS adds transport encryption and authentication through TLS.

[SB-0202] TCP
Type: general knowledge
TCP is a connection-oriented transport protocol that provides ordered, reliable delivery of a byte stream between network endpoints.

[SB-0203] DNS
Type: general knowledge
The Domain Name System translates human-readable domain names into information such as IP addresses and is distributed across many servers.

[SB-0204] RAM
Type: general knowledge
Random-access memory is fast working memory used by a computer to hold data and instructions needed by active processes. Its contents are normally lost when power is removed.

[SB-0205] SSD
Type: general knowledge
A solid-state drive stores data in non-volatile flash memory and has no mechanical read/write head. SSDs generally provide lower latency than hard disk drives.

[SB-0206] Virtual machine
Type: general knowledge
A virtual machine emulates a computer environment so that an operating system and applications can run with virtualized hardware resources.

[SB-0207] Containerization
Type: general knowledge
Software containers package applications with their runtime dependencies while typically sharing the host operating system kernel.

[SB-0208] Public-key cryptography
Type: general knowledge
Public-key cryptography uses mathematically related public and private keys. It supports tasks such as encryption, digital signatures and secure key exchange.

[SB-0209] Hash function
Type: general knowledge
A cryptographic hash function maps arbitrary input to a fixed-size digest and is designed to make certain forms of reversal and collision finding computationally difficult.

[SB-0210] Git
Type: general knowledge
Git is a distributed version-control system. Each clone can contain the project history and developers work through commits, branches and merges.

[SB-0211] Relational database
Type: general knowledge
A relational database organizes information into tables of rows and columns and commonly uses keys and relationships to connect records.

[SB-0212] Index in a database
Type: general knowledge
A database index is an auxiliary data structure that can speed up lookup and sorting at the cost of additional storage and update work.

[SB-0213] REST
Type: general knowledge
REST is an architectural style for networked applications emphasizing resources, representations, stateless interactions and a uniform interface.

[SB-0214] API
Type: general knowledge
An application programming interface defines how software components communicate. APIs can expose functions, data structures, network endpoints or other contracts.

[SB-0215] GPU
Type: general knowledge
A graphics processing unit contains many processing units optimized for highly parallel workloads. GPUs are used for graphics, scientific computing and machine learning.

[SB-0216] Unicode
Type: general knowledge
Unicode is a character-encoding standard that assigns code points to characters used by writing systems and symbols around the world.

[SB-0217] Cache
Type: general knowledge
A cache stores copies of data that are expected to be reused so that future access can be faster than retrieving or recomputing the original data.

[SB-0218] Load balancer
Type: general knowledge
A load balancer distributes incoming requests among multiple backend systems to improve capacity, resilience or responsiveness.

[SB-0219] Firewall
Type: general knowledge
A firewall controls network traffic according to configured security rules and can operate at different layers of the network stack.

[SB-0220] Backup
Type: general knowledge
A backup is an independent copy of data kept so information can be restored after accidental deletion, corruption, hardware failure or another incident.

### DOMAIN: SCIENCE ###

[SB-0301] Newton’s first law
Type: general knowledge
Newton’s first law states that an object remains at rest or in uniform straight-line motion unless acted upon by a net external force.

[SB-0302] Conservation of energy
Type: general knowledge
The law of conservation of energy states that energy cannot be created or destroyed in an isolated system, although it can change form.

[SB-0303] Photosynthesis
Type: general knowledge
Photosynthesis uses light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into energy-rich organic compounds. Oxygen is released as a by-product in oxygenic photosynthesis.

[SB-0304] DNA
Type: general knowledge
DNA is a molecule that stores hereditary information in living organisms. Its sequence is built from four main nucleotide bases: adenine, thymine, cytosine and guanine.

[SB-0305] Plate tectonics
Type: general knowledge
Plate tectonics describes the movement of rigid lithospheric plates over Earth’s mantle. Plate interactions produce earthquakes, volcanism, mountain building and seafloor spreading.

[SB-0306] pH scale
Type: general knowledge
The pH scale is a logarithmic measure related to hydrogen ion activity in a solution. Lower pH values are more acidic and higher values more basic.

[SB-0307] Atom
Type: general knowledge
An atom consists of a nucleus containing protons and neutrons surrounded by electrons. The number of protons determines the chemical element.

[SB-0308] Electromagnetic spectrum
Type: general knowledge
The electromagnetic spectrum includes radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X-rays and gamma rays.

[SB-0309] Entropy
Type: general knowledge
Entropy is a thermodynamic state quantity associated with the number of microscopic configurations compatible with a macroscopic state and with energy dispersal.

[SB-0310] Speed of light
Type: general knowledge
The speed of light in vacuum is exactly 299,792,458 metres per second by definition of the metre.

[SB-0311] Water molecule
Type: general knowledge
A water molecule contains two hydrogen atoms bonded to one oxygen atom. Its bent geometry contributes to water’s polarity.

[SB-0312] Periodic table
Type: general knowledge
The periodic table arranges chemical elements by atomic number and groups elements with recurring chemical properties into columns.

[SB-0313] Evolution by natural selection
Type: general knowledge
Natural selection changes the frequency of heritable traits when individuals with some traits leave more surviving offspring than others in a given environment.

[SB-0314] Greenhouse effect
Type: general knowledge
The greenhouse effect occurs when atmospheric gases absorb and re-emit outgoing infrared radiation, warming Earth’s surface and lower atmosphere.

[SB-0315] Sound waves
Type: general knowledge
Sound in air is a mechanical pressure wave. Its propagation requires a material medium and therefore sound does not travel through a perfect vacuum.

[SB-0316] Friction
Type: general knowledge
Friction is a force that resists relative motion or attempted motion between surfaces in contact.

[SB-0317] Density
Type: general knowledge
Density is mass per unit volume. Two objects of the same size can have different masses if their densities differ.

[SB-0318] Acceleration
Type: general knowledge
Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity with time. A change in speed, direction or both constitutes acceleration.

[SB-0319] Isotope
Type: general knowledge
Isotopes are atoms of the same element with the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons.

[SB-0320] Catalyst
Type: general knowledge
A catalyst increases the rate of a chemical reaction by providing a lower-energy reaction pathway and is regenerated in the overall process.

### DOMAIN: PROGRAMMING ###

[SB-0401] Python list
Type: general knowledge
A Python list is an ordered, mutable collection. Items can be accessed by index and lists can contain values of different types.

[SB-0402] Python tuple
Type: general knowledge
A Python tuple is an ordered collection that is generally immutable after creation.

[SB-0403] Python dictionary
Type: general knowledge
A Python dictionary stores key-value pairs. Keys must be hashable and lookups are normally efficient.

[SB-0404] Python set
Type: general knowledge
A Python set is an unordered collection of unique hashable values and supports operations such as union, intersection and difference.

[SB-0405] Function return value
Type: general knowledge
A function can return a value to its caller. In Python, a function that reaches the end without an explicit return statement returns None.

[SB-0406] Class
Type: general knowledge
A class defines structure and behavior for objects. Individual objects are instances of the class.

[SB-0407] Inheritance
Type: general knowledge
Inheritance allows a class to derive behavior and attributes from another class, supporting reuse and specialization.

[SB-0408] Exception
Type: general knowledge
An exception represents an abnormal condition during program execution. Python uses try and except blocks to handle exceptions.

[SB-0409] List comprehension
Type: general knowledge
A list comprehension creates a list from an iterable using a compact expression that can also include filtering conditions.

[SB-0410] Lambda expression
Type: general knowledge
A Python lambda expression creates a small anonymous function consisting of a single expression.

[SB-0411] Recursion
Type: general knowledge
Recursion occurs when a function solves a problem by calling itself on smaller or simpler instances. A base case is needed to stop the recursion.

[SB-0412] Big O notation
Type: general knowledge
Big O notation describes an asymptotic upper bound on how a resource such as execution time or memory grows with input size.

[SB-0413] Stack data structure
Type: general knowledge
A stack follows a last-in, first-out access pattern. Push adds an item and pop removes the most recently added item.

[SB-0414] Queue data structure
Type: general knowledge
A queue usually follows a first-in, first-out access pattern, where items are added at one end and removed from the other.

[SB-0415] Binary search
Type: general knowledge
Binary search repeatedly halves a sorted search interval and therefore runs in logarithmic time with respect to the number of items.

[SB-0416] Unit test
Type: general knowledge
A unit test checks a small unit of software behavior in isolation or near isolation. Good unit tests are repeatable and make failures easy to diagnose.

[SB-0417] Dependency injection
Type: general knowledge
Dependency injection provides required collaborators from outside an object or function instead of constructing them internally, which can improve testability and flexibility.

[SB-0418] Pure function
Type: general knowledge
A pure function has no externally visible side effects and returns the same output whenever it receives the same inputs.

[SB-0419] Mutable object
Type: general knowledge
A mutable object can be changed after creation. Python lists and dictionaries are common mutable built-in types.

[SB-0420] Iterator
Type: general knowledge
An iterator produces a sequence of values one at a time and keeps track of traversal state between requests for the next value.

### DOMAIN: ASTRONOMY ###

[SB-0501] Solar System
Type: general knowledge
The Solar System consists of the Sun and the objects gravitationally bound to it, including eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids and comets.

[SB-0502] Mercury
Type: general knowledge
Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest of the eight planets in the Solar System.

[SB-0503] Venus
Type: general knowledge
Venus has a dense carbon-dioxide-rich atmosphere and a strong greenhouse effect, producing extremely high surface temperatures.

[SB-0504] Earth
Type: general knowledge
Earth is the third planet from the Sun and has abundant liquid water on its surface.

[SB-0505] Mars
Type: general knowledge
Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. Iron oxides in its surface material contribute to its reddish appearance.

[SB-0506] Jupiter
Type: general knowledge
Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System and is composed primarily of hydrogen and helium.

[SB-0507] Saturn
Type: general knowledge
Saturn is a gas giant famous for a broad ring system made largely of ice particles with rock and dust.

[SB-0508] Uranus
Type: general knowledge
Uranus is an ice giant with an extreme axial tilt, so its rotation axis lies nearly in the plane of its orbit.

[SB-0509] Neptune
Type: general knowledge
Neptune is the eighth major planet from the Sun and has very strong atmospheric winds.

[SB-0510] Milky Way
Type: general knowledge
The Milky Way is the galaxy that contains the Solar System. It is a barred spiral galaxy containing hundreds of billions of stars.

[SB-0511] Light-year
Type: general knowledge
A light-year is a unit of distance equal to the distance light travels in vacuum in one Julian year.

[SB-0512] Black hole
Type: general knowledge
A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity is strong enough that, beyond the event horizon, no signal can escape to distant observers.

[SB-0513] Supernova
Type: general knowledge
A supernova is a powerful stellar explosion that can occur through more than one physical mechanism, including core collapse of a massive star.

[SB-0514] Red giant
Type: general knowledge
A red giant is an evolved star with a large radius and relatively cool surface temperature compared with its earlier main-sequence state.

[SB-0515] White dwarf
Type: general knowledge
A white dwarf is a dense stellar remnant supported mainly by electron degeneracy pressure.

[SB-0516] Exoplanet
Type: general knowledge
An exoplanet is a planet orbiting a star other than the Sun.

[SB-0517] Galaxy
Type: general knowledge
A galaxy is a gravitationally bound system of stars, stellar remnants, gas, dust and dark matter.

[SB-0518] Nebula
Type: general knowledge
A nebula is an interstellar cloud of gas and dust. Some nebulae are sites of star formation while others are remnants of stellar evolution.

[SB-0519] Orbit
Type: general knowledge
An orbit is the curved path of one object around another under gravity. Stable orbital motion continually changes the direction of velocity.

[SB-0520] Telescope
Type: general knowledge
A telescope collects electromagnetic radiation from distant objects and forms or records information that can reveal properties too faint or small for unaided observation.

### DOMAIN: ECONOMICS ###

[SB-0801] Supply and demand
Type: general knowledge
In a competitive market, supply describes quantities producers are willing to sell at different prices while demand describes quantities consumers are willing to buy.

[SB-0802] Inflation
Type: general knowledge
Inflation is a sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services, reducing the purchasing power of money when incomes do not rise equally.

[SB-0803] GDP
Type: general knowledge
Gross domestic product measures the market value of final goods and services produced within a country during a specified period.

[SB-0804] Opportunity cost
Type: general knowledge
Opportunity cost is the value of the best alternative forgone when a choice is made.

[SB-0805] Comparative advantage
Type: general knowledge
Comparative advantage explains gains from specialization when parties focus on activities with lower opportunity costs and trade with one another.

[SB-0806] Interest rate
Type: general knowledge
An interest rate is the price of borrowing money or the return earned on lending or saving, usually expressed as a percentage over time.

[SB-0807] Central bank
Type: general knowledge
A central bank manages important parts of a country’s monetary system and may set policy rates, issue currency, manage reserves and support financial stability.

[SB-0808] Fiscal policy
Type: general knowledge
Fiscal policy uses government spending and taxation to influence economic activity and public finances.

[SB-0809] Monetary policy
Type: general knowledge
Monetary policy is the use of central-bank tools to influence financial conditions, inflation and economic activity.

[SB-0810] Unemployment rate
Type: general knowledge
The unemployment rate is the share of the labor force that is without work, available for work and actively seeking employment according to the relevant statistical definition.

[SB-0811] Productivity
Type: general knowledge
Productivity measures output produced per unit of input, such as output per worker or per hour worked.

[SB-0812] Exchange rate
Type: general knowledge
An exchange rate states how much of one currency can be exchanged for another.

[SB-0813] Budget deficit
Type: general knowledge
A budget deficit occurs when government expenditures exceed revenues during a given period.

[SB-0814] Public debt
Type: general knowledge
Public debt is the accumulated outstanding borrowing of government entities, subject to the definitions used in a particular jurisdiction.

[SB-0815] Market liquidity
Type: general knowledge
Liquidity describes how easily an asset can be bought or sold without causing a large change in its market price.

[SB-0816] Diversification
Type: general knowledge
Diversification spreads exposure across different assets or activities so that performance does not depend as heavily on a single source of risk.

[SB-0817] Externality
Type: general knowledge
An externality is a cost or benefit from an activity that affects third parties and is not fully reflected in the market transaction.

[SB-0818] Price elasticity
Type: general knowledge
Price elasticity of demand measures how responsive the quantity demanded is to a change in price.

[SB-0819] Human capital
Type: general knowledge
Human capital refers to knowledge, skills, health and other personal attributes that contribute to productive capacity.

[SB-0820] Recession
Type: general knowledge
A recession is a broad decline in economic activity lasting more than a brief fluctuation; exact definitions and dating methods vary across institutions.

### DOMAIN: ENVIRONMENT ###

[SB-0901] Carbon cycle
Type: general knowledge
The carbon cycle describes exchanges of carbon among the atmosphere, oceans, living organisms, soils, rocks and sediments.

[SB-0902] Nitrogen cycle
Type: general knowledge
The nitrogen cycle moves nitrogen among the atmosphere, organisms, soil and water through processes including fixation, nitrification, assimilation and denitrification.

[SB-0903] Biodiversity
Type: general knowledge
Biodiversity refers to variation within species, between species and among ecosystems.

[SB-0904] Wetland
Type: general knowledge
A wetland is an ecosystem where water saturation strongly influences soil conditions and the organisms that live there.

[SB-0905] Coral bleaching
Type: general knowledge
Coral bleaching occurs when stressed corals lose or expel much of the symbiotic algae that provide them with energy and color.

[SB-0906] Ocean acidification
Type: general knowledge
Ocean acidification is the long-term decrease in seawater pH caused mainly by absorption of human-produced carbon dioxide.

[SB-0907] Renewable energy
Type: general knowledge
Renewable energy comes from sources replenished naturally on human timescales, such as sunlight, wind, flowing water and geothermal heat.

[SB-0908] Solar power
Type: general knowledge
Solar photovoltaic cells convert light directly into electricity through semiconductor materials.

[SB-0909] Wind power
Type: general knowledge
Wind turbines convert kinetic energy in moving air into rotational mechanical energy and then electricity.

[SB-0910] Hydroelectric power
Type: general knowledge
Hydroelectric generation uses moving or falling water to drive turbines connected to electrical generators.

[SB-0911] Eutrophication
Type: general knowledge
Eutrophication occurs when excess nutrients stimulate biological production in water, potentially leading to oxygen depletion and ecosystem disruption.

[SB-0912] Deforestation
Type: general knowledge
Deforestation is the conversion of forested land to other uses and can affect biodiversity, carbon storage, soil and local water cycles.

[SB-0913] Reforestation
Type: general knowledge
Reforestation is the re-establishment of forest cover on land that was previously forested.

[SB-0914] Soil erosion
Type: general knowledge
Soil erosion is the removal and transport of soil by water, wind or other processes and can be accelerated by land-use practices.

[SB-0915] Invasive species
Type: general knowledge
An invasive species is a non-native organism that spreads and causes ecological, economic or other harm in the introduced region.

[SB-0916] Composting
Type: general knowledge
Composting is controlled biological decomposition of organic material into a stable soil-like amendment.

[SB-0917] Circular economy
Type: general knowledge
A circular economy aims to keep products and materials in use longer through durability, reuse, repair, remanufacturing and recycling.

[SB-0918] Habitat fragmentation
Type: general knowledge
Habitat fragmentation divides continuous habitat into smaller isolated patches, which can affect movement, breeding and long-term population viability.

[SB-0919] Ecological succession
Type: general knowledge
Ecological succession is the change in community composition over time following disturbance or the creation of new habitat.

[SB-0920] Watershed
Type: general knowledge
A watershed or drainage basin is an area of land where precipitation drains toward a common outlet such as a river, lake or sea.

### DOMAIN: LITERATURE ###

[SB-1001] Narrator
Type: general knowledge
A narrator is the voice or perspective through which a story is presented. The narrator is not necessarily identical to the author.

[SB-1002] First-person narration
Type: general knowledge
First-person narration uses a narrator who refers to themselves as I or we and presents events from their own perspective.

[SB-1003] Third-person limited
Type: general knowledge
Third-person limited narration follows characters using third-person pronouns while restricting access mainly to one character’s perceptions or thoughts at a time.

[SB-1004] Unreliable narrator
Type: general knowledge
An unreliable narrator presents an account whose accuracy, completeness or interpretation the reader has reason to doubt.

[SB-1005] Metaphor
Type: general knowledge
A metaphor describes one thing in terms of another without using a literal identity claim, creating an implied comparison or conceptual mapping.

[SB-1006] Simile
Type: general knowledge
A simile makes an explicit comparison, commonly using words such as like or as.

[SB-1007] Foreshadowing
Type: general knowledge
Foreshadowing introduces details that anticipate or prepare the reader for events that occur later in a narrative.

[SB-1008] Motif
Type: general knowledge
A motif is a recurring image, phrase, idea, object or situation that contributes to a work’s themes or structure.

[SB-1009] Theme
Type: general knowledge
A theme is a recurring or underlying idea explored through a literary work rather than a simple one-sentence moral.

[SB-1010] Allegory
Type: general knowledge
An allegory is a narrative in which characters, events or settings systematically correspond to another level of meaning.

[SB-1011] Tragedy
Type: general knowledge
Tragedy is a dramatic or narrative form centered on serious conflict and suffering, often involving irreversible loss.

[SB-1012] Comedy
Type: general knowledge
Comedy is a broad dramatic or literary mode that uses humor, incongruity or social conflict and often moves toward reconciliation or resolution.

[SB-1013] Epic
Type: general knowledge
An epic is a long narrative, traditionally in verse, dealing with heroic figures and events important to a culture or community.

[SB-1014] Protagonist
Type: general knowledge
The protagonist is a central character whose goals or conflicts organize much of a narrative.

[SB-1015] Antagonist
Type: general knowledge
An antagonist is a character, force or condition that opposes the protagonist or creates central conflict.

[SB-1016] Setting
Type: general knowledge
Setting includes the time, place, social environment and physical surroundings in which a narrative occurs.

[SB-1017] Plot
Type: general knowledge
Plot is the structured sequence and causal organization of events in a narrative.

[SB-1018] Irony
Type: general knowledge
Irony involves a meaningful contrast between appearance and reality, expectation and outcome, or literal words and intended meaning.

[SB-1019] Alliteration
Type: general knowledge
Alliteration is repetition of similar initial consonant sounds in nearby words.

[SB-1020] Free verse
Type: general knowledge
Free verse is poetry that does not follow a fixed metrical pattern or regular rhyme scheme, though it may still use deliberate rhythm and sound.

Type: general knowledge

[SB-1104] Integral
Type: general knowledge

[SB-1105] Vector
Type: general knowledge

[SB-1106] Matrix

[SB-1107] Probability
Type: general knowledge
Probability quantifies uncertainty using values between zero and one, where zero represents impossibility and one represents certainty in the mathematical model.

[SB-1108] Mean
Type: general knowledge
The arithmetic mean of a set of numbers is the sum of the values divided by the number of values.

[SB-1109] Median
Type: general knowledge
The median is the middle value of an ordered dataset, or the mean of the two middle values when the number of observations is even.

[SB-1110] Standard deviation
Type: general knowledge
Standard deviation measures the typical spread of values around the mean and is the square root of variance.

[SB-1111] Linear equation
Type: general knowledge
A linear equation contains variables only to the first power and represents a straight line in two dimensions when written with two variables.

[SB-1112] Quadratic equation
Type: general knowledge
A quadratic equation in one variable has highest exponent two and can have zero, one or two real roots.

[SB-1113] Logarithm
Type: general knowledge
A logarithm answers the question of what exponent is required to produce a given value from a specified base.

[SB-1114] Exponent
Type: general knowledge
An exponent indicates repeated multiplication in elementary arithmetic and extends to broader definitions for real and complex powers.

[SB-1115] Factorial
Type: general knowledge
For a positive integer n, n factorial is the product of all positive integers from one through n; zero factorial is defined as one.

[SB-1116] Permutation
Type: general knowledge
A permutation is an ordered arrangement of objects. Changing the order creates a different permutation.

[SB-1117] Combination
Type: general knowledge
A combination is a selection where order does not matter.

[SB-1118] Euclidean geometry
Type: general knowledge
Euclidean geometry studies points, lines, planes, distances and angles under the classical Euclidean postulates.

[SB-1119] Trigonometry
Type: general knowledge
Trigonometry studies relationships among angles and side lengths, especially through functions such as sine, cosine and tangent.

[SB-1120] Set theory
Type: general knowledge
Set theory studies collections of objects and operations such as union, intersection, difference and complement.
